# How to share photos with the family

My notes from the lesson, in my own words.

1. Open the Photos app and find the picture you want to send.
2. Tap the Share button. It looks like a square with an arrow pointing up.
3. Choose Messages to send it as a text, or Mail to send it as an email.
4. Type the name of the person, for example Priya, and press Send.
5. To send lots of photos at once, press Select first, tap each picture, then Share.

Tip: if a photo is very large it may take a minute to send. Wait for the little tick to appear.
