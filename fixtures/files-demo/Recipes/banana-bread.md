# Banana bread

Nana's recipe, written out so I don't lose it.

## Ingredients
- 3 ripe bananas, mashed
- 75 g melted butter
- 150 g sugar
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- a pinch of salt
- 190 g plain flour

## Method
1. Heat the oven to 175 C. Butter a loaf tin.
2. Mix the mashed bananas with the melted butter.
3. Stir in the sugar, egg and vanilla, then the baking soda and salt.
4. Fold in the flour until just mixed.
5. Pour into the tin and bake for about 55 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
6. Leave to cool for ten minutes before slicing. It keeps for three days in a tin.
